## Show HN (Hacker News)

**Title:** Show HN: Chauncy – local semantic search over your PDFs, email, and notes

**Body:**

I built Chauncy because I kept losing things I had already saved. Desktop search finds filenames and keywords. Note tools ask you to maintain a graph. Chatbots want another upload.

Chauncy is a Windows desktop app (local-first) that:

- Ingests PDFs, Word, email (.eml/.mbox/IMAP), Markdown, HTML, URLs, notes
- Indexes with local embeddings (hybrid semantic + keyword search)
- Surfaces related material without manual linking
- Optional AI synthesis via your own API key or Ollama — core search works offline

No account, no telemetry, no autonomous agent behavior. One-time purchase ($99 early adopter for first 100).

Site: https://chauncy.dev  
I’m especially interested in feedback on first-run friction and whether the “search by meaning” demos match how you actually hunt for old material.

If your vault/archive is large and you mostly remember *what something was about* rather than the title, you might care about this.
