### SECTION: Privacy

**Heading:**
Private by design. Not by policy.

**Body:**
Most tools promise privacy in their terms of service. Chauncy delivers it by architecture.

Your documents, your search index, and all discovered connections live in a database on your computer. Chauncy has no servers to breach, no account to compromise, and no telemetry to disable.

When you use an AI feature — summarization, deeper analysis — relevant excerpts are sent to the API provider you choose. You select the provider, you supply the key, and their privacy policy governs that data. Chauncy is transparent about exactly when this happens.

If you need fully offline AI analysis, point Chauncy at a local model. It supports that too.

## PAGE: FAQ (/faq)

---

**Heading:**
Questions

---

**Q: What formats does Chauncy support?**
PDF, email (including IMAP mailbox sync), Markdown, Microsoft Word, PowerPoint, plain text, HTML, web URLs via the clipper, typed notes, and voice notes. If you have documents in a format not listed, contact us — we're adding formats based on demand.

**Q: Does Chauncy require an internet connection?**
No. All core features — search, document ingestion, automatic connections — run entirely on your machine. An internet connection is only needed when you use an AI feature (summarization, deeper analysis), because those requests go to the API provider you've configured.

**Q: Wait — Chauncy opens in my browser. Is it a web app?**
No. Chauncy is a desktop application. It uses your browser as its interface for technical reasons, but it runs entirely on your computer. No data is sent to any Chauncy server. There is no Chauncy server.

**Q: Do I need an AI API key to use Chauncy?**
No. AI features are optional. Search, ingestion, and automatic document connections all work without an API key. The AI layer adds capabilities like natural language summarization when you want them.

**Q: Which AI providers does Chauncy support?**
OpenAI, Anthropic, Groq, and any provider with a compatible API endpoint. If you run a local model via LM Studio or a similar tool, you can point Chauncy at it using the custom endpoint setting.

**Q: Who is Chauncy for?**
Researchers, writers, analysts, consultants, and anyone managing a large personal document collection who doesn't want to spend their time organizing it. If you've ever thought "I know I saved something about this somewhere," Chauncy is for you.

**Q: Who is Chauncy not for?**
Chauncy is not designed for compliance-regulated environments. If you operate under HIPAA, attorney-client privilege requirements, federal grant data handling rules, or similar regulatory frameworks, Chauncy is not the right tool. We're not built for that use case and make no claims to support it.

**Q: Does Chauncy sync across devices?**
Not currently. Chauncy is a single-machine application. Your document collection lives on the machine where you installed it.

**Q: Is macOS supported?**
Not at launch. macOS support is in active development. Join the waitlist at chauncy.dev/waitlist and we'll notify you when it's ready. Launch pricing applies to waitlist customers.

**Q: Where do my documents go when I add them?**
Into a local database on your machine. The full text, metadata, and vector embeddings are stored there. Nothing leaves your machine unless you use an AI feature.

**Q: Can I move my Chauncy database to a different machine?**
Yes. Chauncy stores everything in a single database file you can locate in Settings. Copy it to your new machine and you're done. Documentation for migration is included.

**Q: What happens if I want to stop using Chauncy?**
Your documents are yours. You can export everything and Chauncy stores nothing externally to delete.

**Chauncy Privacy Policy**
*Last updated: [DATE]*

---

**Overview**

Chauncy is a desktop application. It runs on your computer. Your documents, your search index, and all data Chauncy generates from your documents are stored locally on your machine.

Chauncy does not have a user account system. Chauncy does not collect telemetry. Chauncy does not operate servers that receive your data.

This policy describes the limited circumstances in which data leaves your machine and what governs it when it does.

---

**Data Stored Locally**

The following data is stored on your machine only, in a local database:

- The full text of documents you import
- Metadata associated with those documents (filename, date, source)
- Vector embeddings generated from your documents
- Annotations you add to documents
- Your Chauncy settings and configuration

This data does not leave your machine through any Chauncy mechanism.

---

**AI Features and Third-Party Providers**

Chauncy's AI features — including natural language query interpretation and document summarization — are optional. They require you to configure a third-party API provider (such as OpenAI, Anthropic, or Groq) and supply your own API key.

When you use an AI feature, Chauncy sends relevant excerpts from your documents to the API endpoint you have configured. This transmission is governed entirely by the privacy policy of the provider you have chosen, not by this policy.

Chauncy is transparent about when AI features are active. You are in control of which provider you use and when AI features are invoked.

If you require fully local AI processing, Chauncy supports configuration of a local model endpoint (e.g., LM Studio). In that case, no data leaves your machine for AI processing.

---

**Purchase and Payment**

Chauncy uses Stripe for payment processing. When you purchase Chauncy, your payment information is handled entirely by Stripe. Chauncy does not receive or store your credit card details.

Stripe's privacy policy governs data collected during purchase: [stripe.com/privacy](https://stripe.com/privacy)

The only information Chauncy retains from your purchase is your email address, used to deliver your receipt and download link.

---

**Email Communications**

If you join the macOS waitlist, your email address is stored for the sole purpose of notifying you when macOS support is available. It will not be used for marketing or shared with third parties.

---

**Compliance-Regulated Environments**

Chauncy is not designed for use in compliance-regulated environments, including but not limited to those governed by HIPAA, attorney-client privilege requirements, or federal grant data handling regulations. Do not use Chauncy to store or process data subject to these requirements.

---

**Changes to This Policy**

If this policy changes materially, we will update the date at the top of this page. We will not reduce your privacy protections without notice.
