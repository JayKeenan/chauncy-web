# Chauncy Return Policy

*Last updated: March 2026*

---

## 14-Day Money-Back Guarantee

If you purchase Chauncy and it isn't right for you, request a full refund within 14 days of your purchase date. No explanation required.

---

## How to Request a Refund

Email [support@chauncy.dev](mailto:support@chauncy.dev) with the subject line "Refund Request" and include the email address used at purchase. We will process your refund within 3-5 business days. Refunds are returned to the original payment method via Stripe.

---

## After 14 Days

We do not offer refunds after 14 days from the date of purchase. If you are experiencing a technical problem, contact us — we will do our best to resolve it.

---

## Questions

Contact [support@chauncy.dev](mailto:support@chauncy.dev).
